# Design

This document outlines the design guidelines for the CatchAnything app.

## Color Scheme

*   **Primary Color:** #4CAF50 (Green)
*   **Accent Color:** #FFC107 (Amber)
*   **Background Color:** #FFFFFF (White)
*   **Text Color:** #212121 (Black)

## Typography

*   **Font Family:** Roboto
*   **Headline Font Size:** 24sp
*   **Body Font Size:** 16sp

## UI Components

*   **Buttons:** ElevatedButton with a green background and white text.
*   **Text Fields:** Outlined text fields with a green border.
*   **Cards:** Cards with a white background and a subtle shadow.
